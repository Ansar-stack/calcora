What's the difference between playback speed and fast forward?

Playback speed increases audio pitch while maintaining all content, whereas fast forward typically skips content. Our speed multiplier time calculator focuses on playback speed adjustments where every second of content is preserved but played faster. This makes it more accurate than estimating fast forward time, as it calculates exact video time after increasing playback speed without missing any material.

How do I calculate time saved for an entire playlist or course?

For multiple videos, calculate each video separately using our video acceleration calculator, then sum the results. For example, if you have a 10-video course where each video is 30 minutes, you'd calculate time at 1.5x speed for one video (20 minutes), then multiply by 10. Our playback speed calculator can help you estimate total time savings for entire learning modules when used systematically.
