WHP to HP Calculator: Convert Wheel Horsepower to Engine HP

Use our free WHP to HP calculator to convert wheel horsepower to engine horsepower instantly. Simply enter your WHP value, select your drivetrain type (FWD, RWD, or AWD), and optionally add custom drivetrain loss percentage. Get accurate engine power calculations for performance tuning and automotive analysis.

How to Use This WHP to HP Calculator

1. Enter your Wheel Horsepower (WHP) value - this is power measured at the wheels from dyno testing. 2. Select your drivetrain type: choose FWD for front-wheel drive (15% loss), RWD for rear-wheel drive (20% loss), or AWD/4WD for all-wheel drive (25% loss). 3. For custom setups, select 'Add Custom Drivetrain Loss' and enter your specific percentage. 4. Click 'Calculate HP' to convert WHP to engine horsepower. The calculator uses the formula: HP = WHP ÷ (1 - drivetrain loss % / 100). Use this tool for accurate wheel power to engine power conversion in automotive performance analysis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a WHP to HP Calculator?

A WHP to HP calculator is a wheel horsepower to engine horsepower converter that estimates engine power based on wheel power measurements. This wheel power to engine power calculator accounts for drivetrain losses between the engine and wheels. Use this whp to horsepower converter for accurate power calculations in automotive performance tuning.

How does this WHP to HP conversion work?

This wheel hp to engine hp calculator uses drivetrain loss percentages to calculate engine horsepower from wheel horsepower. The formula divides WHP by (1 - drivetrain loss percentage/100). Our whp to horsepower converter includes preset values for FWD (15%), RWD (20%), and AWD (25%) vehicles, plus custom loss options.

What's the difference between WHP and HP?

HP (horsepower) measures engine output at the crankshaft, while WHP (wheel horsepower) measures power delivered to the wheels after drivetrain losses. This wheel power to engine power calculator converts WHP to HP by accounting for these losses. The whp to bhp conversion considers power absorbed by transmission, differential, and other components.

Why do I need to select drivetrain type?

Drivetrain type significantly affects power loss in wheel hp to engine hp calculations. Front-wheel drive (FWD) typically loses 15%, rear-wheel drive (RWD) loses 20%, and all-wheel drive (AWD/4WD) loses 25% in this wheel power to engine power calculator. Selecting the correct type ensures accurate whp to horsepower conversion results.
