Osu PP Calculator - Calculate Your Performance Points Online

Want to know how your latest osu! play stacks up? Our Osu PP Calculator helps you figure that out. Just enter your score, the map's star difficulty, your highest combo, and any mods you used (like Hard Rock or Double Time). The calculator instantly gives you an estimated Performance Points (PP) value, helping you track your progress and understand what affects your ranking. It's the fastest way to calculate osu pp online and see how you're improving.

How to use Osu PP Calculator?

Using the Osu PP Calculator is simple and fast. To calculate your performance points online, you'll need a few details from your play. Start by entering your total score from the map. Then, input the star difficulty rating of that beatmap. Next, add your highest combo achieved during the play. Finally, select any mods you had active (this is important as mods like HR or DT significantly affect PP). Once you enter these, the tool provides an accurate osu pp estimation and a detailed osu pp calculation breakdown. This lets you instantly convert your osu score to performance points. Whether you're a competitive player wanting to check osu pp for specific mod combinations, using it as an osu star rating calculator to understand map difficulty, or just need a general performance point calculator to track your growth, this tool makes understanding your performance points easy, precise, and insightful.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Osu PP Calculator?

It's a specialized online tool designed for osu! players to estimate their Performance Points (PP) for a specific play. The Osu PP Calculator lets you calculate osu performance points online by entering key details from your gameplay: your score, the map's difficulty rating, your maximum combo, and any active mods. It processes this information to give you an accurate osu pp estimation, so you can track your performance points over time, understand your strengths and weaknesses, and strategically improve your gameplay and ranking.

Is this calculator free to use?

Absolutely! The Osu PP Calculator is completely free and always will be. You can calculate osu pp online as many times as you want, check osu pp for different mod combinations, and track your osu performance points online without any charges, subscriptions, or hidden fees. What inputs are needed for this calculator?

You need four main pieces of information from your play session. First, your total score on the beatmap. Second, the star difficulty rating of that specific map. Third, the highest combo you achieved (your max combo). And fourth, which mods, if any, you had active during the play (like No Fail, Hidden, or Double Time). The osu pp calculator uses these inputs together to provide a fast and precise osu pp estimation for your specific gameplay scenario.

How accurate is the PP estimation?

The osu pp calculator provides a very precise osu pp estimation based on the standard PP calculation formulas used by the osu! ranking system. It uses your score, combo, difficulty, and mods to simulate the calculation. While it's an estimate (the official server calculation has some additional factors), it's highly reliable for seeing how your osu score translates to performance points in real time and for comparing the potential value of different plays.

Can I use it on any device?

Yes! This performance point calculator is fully responsive and works perfectly on any device. Whether you're on a desktop computer, a laptop, a tablet, or your smartphone, you can calculate osu pp online anytime.
