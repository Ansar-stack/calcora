What formula does this calculator use?

Our hypotenuse formula calculator applies the classic and universally trusted Pythagoras theorem: c = √(a² + b²), where 'c' is the hypotenuse and 'a' and 'b' are the other two sides. This is the standard method to calculate the hypotenuse of a right triangle, ensuring mathematically precise and reliable results for any right triangle dimensions you provide.
