What is a Factorial Calculator?

It's a specialized mathematical tool designed to compute one of the most important functions in combinatorics and probability. A factorial calculator quickly computes the factorial of any non-negative integer n (written as n!). The factorial represents the product of all positive integers from 1 to n. For example, 5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120. This easy factorial calculator online helps students learning about permutations and combinations, professionals in statistics and computer science, and anyone dealing with probability problems get accurate results without the tedious manual multiplication that becomes impractical with numbers larger than about 10.
