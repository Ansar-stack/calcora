How do I find the percentage of a number?

Finding the percentage of a number is simple with our tool. You need two pieces of information: the total (or whole) number and the specific part of that total you're evaluating. Enter the total number into the first field of this Percentage Calculator. Then, enter the specific part (the obtained value) into the second field. Click calculate. The tool will instantly compute and display the result, showing you exactly what percentage the part is of the whole. For example, to find what percentage 75 out of 200 is, you enter 200 as the total and 75 as the obtained value to get 37.5%.
